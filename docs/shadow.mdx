# Shadow

The tokens come from the [`@gls-nxt/design-tokens`](https://github.com/gls-nxt/design-tokens) package.

The shadow tokens are colours, from elevation level 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest). Components combine them with their own offsets and blur.
